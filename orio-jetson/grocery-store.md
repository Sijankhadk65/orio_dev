The store is open Monday through Saturday from 8am to 10pm, and Sunday from 9am to 8pm.

Fresh produce is right by the entrance, on the left — fruits closest to the door, vegetables further in.

The bakery is at the front of the store, next to the produce section, with fresh bread baked every morning.

The deli counter is at the back of the store, along the rear wall, and closes 30 minutes before the store does.

Dairy and eggs are in aisle 1, along the back-left wall, next to the deli.

Frozen foods are in aisles 2 and 3, including frozen vegetables, meals, and ice cream.

Canned goods and soups are in aisle 4.

Dry goods — pasta, rice, cereal, and baking supplies — are in aisles 5 and 6.

Snacks and beverages are in aisle 7, including chips, soda, and bottled water.

Household supplies and cleaning products are in aisle 8.

The pharmacy is in the back-right corner of the store, open the same hours as the store except it closes an hour earlier.

Checkout lanes are at the front of the store; self-checkout is on the far right for customers with 15 items or fewer.

Restrooms are located near the front entrance, past the customer service desk.

The customer service desk, near the entrance, handles returns, refunds, and gift cards.

Returns are accepted within 30 days with a receipt; perishable items cannot be returned once opened.

The store accepts cash, credit and debit cards, and mobile payments like Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Reusable bags are available for purchase at checkout for $0.10 each; customers can also bring their own.

Free parking is available in the lot in front of the store, including designated spots near the entrance for accessibility.

The store has a loyalty program — customers can sign up for free at the customer service desk to get member discounts and weekly coupons.

Shopping carts and baskets are available near the entrance; carts can also be returned to corrals throughout the parking lot.
